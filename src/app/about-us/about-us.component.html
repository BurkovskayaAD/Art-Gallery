Press About Us

Like any living organism, the museum is constantly growing, replenishing its funds. The collection of Belarusian and Russian art, Western European art, the art of the countries of the East, contemporary Belarusian art now numbers more than 30 thousand works.

A museum is not only paintings, it is also people, thanks to whose efforts in different periods of history new expositions are created, exhibitions are organized, books and catalogs are published, works of art are replenished, stored, researched, restored and promoted. Now the staff of the museum numbers about three hundred people who adequately continue the traditions of their senior museum colleagues.
